Implement TTLCache in cache.py: a thread-safe LRU cache where each entry also expires after a time to live, with an injectable clock. Expired entries must be purged before any live entry is evicted for capacity. get() marks recency but does not extend the TTL, `in` and len() must ignore expired entries without touching recency, overwrite resets the TTL, and stats() must count hits, misses, capacity evictions and expirations exactly. The docstring and test_cache.py define the full contract, including concurrency. Make `python3 -m unittest` pass.
