Write a safe arithmetic expression evaluator in calc.py without eval, exec, compile or the ast module. It needs its own tokenizer and recursive-descent parser with correct precedence for + - * / %, a right-associative ** that binds tighter than unary minus (so -2 ** 2 is -4), stacked unary operators, decimals and scientific notation, named variables, and min/max/abs with arity checks. Every malformed expression, unknown name, wrong arity and division or modulo by zero must raise CalcError. test_calc.py is the full contract, including edge cases. Make `python3 -m unittest` pass.
